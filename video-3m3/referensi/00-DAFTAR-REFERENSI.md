## Spesifikasi yang terbaca
- Kapasitas produk **3 m³/hari**, recovery **12%**, tekanan operasi **42 bar**, flux **17 LMH**
- Alur: air laut → **feed pump SS304 0,5 HP** → (pipa 1") → **tangki FRP 1054 sand filter** → **tangki FRP 1054 activated carbon / carbon block** → **cartridge 20"** → injeksi **antiscalant/biocide** (dosing pump + tangki) → **HP pump plunger SS316 (20 Lpm, 60 bar)** → valve HP + pressure gauge 42 bar → **membran SW30-4040, housing FRP 4"** (1 elemen) → conductivity meter + flowmeter produk → **produk ke tangki (maks. 10 m tinggi)**
- Konsentrat: pressure gauge → needle/flow valve + flush valve (bypass) → flowmeter konsentrat → drain
- Skid **SS304 hollow 40×40**, ± 2,2 × 0,8 × 1,6 m, plat perforated, kaki adjustable + pad karet
- Panel kontrol: lampu indikator (hijau/kuning/merah), display controller, selector, emergency stop, MCB/kontaktor/PS; 2 pressure gauge (PG feed HP, PG concentrate) + 2 rotameter (FI product, FI concentrate)
- Head manual 3-way valve di tiap tangki FRP; pipa backwash/drain tangki

## Perlu dikonfirmasi (ada perbedaan antar referensi)
1. **Cartridge:** P&ID = 1 housing 20" **5 µm**; model 3D = **2× 20 µm + 2× 5 µm**; infografis = 20 µm (carbon block) + 5 µm. (Video kontainer direvisi ke 1 µm.)
2. **Jumlah tangki FRP:** model 3D = 2 (sand + activated carbon); foto produk = 3.
3. **Angka produk di infografis:** tertulis produk 0,36 m³/hari & konsentrat 2,64 m³/hari, tapi dengan 1 elemen SW30-4040 (± 7,4 m²) × 17 LMH ≈ 3 m³/hari produk, dan HP pump 20 Lpm × 12% ≈ 3,5 m³/hari. Kemungkinan **3 m³/hari = produk**, umpan ± 25 m³/hari.
